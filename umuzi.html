Multi-Country Talent Placement: The Social Connect × Umuzi

Building credible employer pipelines from zero across three African markets in 90 days, placing SAP-certified talent at scale through intelligence-led outreach and rigorous pipeline management.

Timeline: 3 months (Dec 2025 to Present)
Markets: South Africa · Kenya · Nigeria
Industry: Tech Talent / SAP Recruitment
Role: Business Development Manager, Talent Placement

Three Markets. One Pipeline.

Simultaneous outreach across sub-Saharan Africa's top tech hiring markets

IT · Consulting · Finance
🇰🇪 Kenya

The Challenge

A formal commercial partnership between The Social Connect and Umuzi needed to rapidly place SAP-certified and tech-adjacent talent across three African markets simultaneously — with no existing employer relationships in any of those regions. The mandate was to build credible pipelines from zero while managing contractual reporting obligations and maintaining quality outreach at scale.

The Approach

Intelligence-First Targeting

Market research was conducted to identify highest-probability employer segments before a single message was sent, eliminating wasted effort and directing energy where it would convert.

AI-Augmented Personalisation

AI tooling was combined with AIDA and PAS copywriting frameworks to deliver personalised, compelling outreach at a volume that would have been impossible to achieve through manual effort alone.

Data-Driven Iteration

Open rates and response rates were tracked continuously and fed back into copy refinement so that every sequence improved over time rather than staying static after launch.

Structured Pipeline Discipline

Every prospect was logged, segmented by industry and hiring readiness, and tracked through a multi-stage funnel with consistent weekly reporting to meet contractual accountability standards.

Candidate-as-Product Thinking

Talent showcase materials were designed to position SAP candidates the way a strong B2B product is presented, meeting corporate procurement expectations rather than functioning as a simple CV collection.

Cross-Border Nuance

Employer communications were adapted for each market to reflect the distinct hiring cultures and business norms across South Africa, Kenya, and Nigeria, all being managed simultaneously.
