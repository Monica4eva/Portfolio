Building a Women-Led Agricultural Brand from Zero

How I transformed B Agro Crop from a startup with no online presence into a recognized sustainable agriculture leader—achieving 10,000+ impressions in 3 weeks and positioning for international expansion while the company empowered 300+ women farmers

Timeline: 16 months (Jul 2024 - Nov 2025)
Industry: Sustainable Agriculture / AgriTech
My Role: Chief Marketing Officer
Location: Remote (Botswana → Ghana)

The Challenge

In July 2024, I was appointed as Chief Marketing Officer for B Agro Crop Ltd, a women-led agricultural processing company in Ziope, Ghana. The company specialized in processing locally grown vegetables into shelf-stable powdered products to reduce post-harvest losses and empower women farmers.

The challenge? Build everything from absolute zero:

  • • No LinkedIn presence, social media strategy, or brand voice
  • • No marketing materials, pitch decks, or investor-ready documentation
  • • Needed to raise GHS 50,000 ($4,500 USD) to complete factory setup
  • • Accepted into ESEA Acceleration Programme requiring comprehensive deliverables
  • • Sister company (Protifai) needed Botswana market research for expansion

My Role

Chief Marketing Officer • Brand Strategist • Investor Relations Lead • Market Research Analyst

Project Showcase
