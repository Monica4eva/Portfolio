The Real Story

She didn't come from money.
She came from mission.

Every founder has a reason. Here's mine.

The Beginning

I always knew I wanted to be an entrepreneur. Or at minimum, build one.

My plan started with babysitting services, then evolved into errands and assistant work. I told myself I would help people with traditional jobs start their dream businesses, I could be their support system. That vision never left me. I fell in love with digital marketing during my first degree and never looked back.

"Marketing became the bridge between my clients' vision and the world that needed to hear about it."

👩‍👧

"I became a mother at 22, juggling school, a side business, and every dream I refused to abandon."

Motherhood + Ambition

Entrepreneurship became the way through, not around.

Being a young single mother didn't shrink my vision, it sharpened it. I needed flexibility, freedom, and financial control. In 2014, I channelled that into founding STAYM (Strength of a Young Mother).

I've helped women across agriculture, edtech, skincare, fashion, hospitality, travel, and coaching go from feeling lost to watching their vision gain meaning as others started to invest in what they were building.

The Pivot That Proved Everything

A global pandemic. A master's degree.
A grant that changed everything.

Here is the thing nobody talks about honestly. When the world stopped in 2020 and most people froze, I made a decision that would define everything that came after. I enrolled in an Executive Master's in Entrepreneurship at the University of Botswana, while managing a consulting business, running an NGO, and raising my daughter as a single mother. No support structure. No gap year. Just a decision to use the chaos as a classroom.

The programme was rigorous and I treated it that way. I graduated with a 4.1 GPA, not because academic performance was the goal, but because I apply that same standard to everything I build. That same year, I received the Tony Elumelu Foundation grant. Not luck. Years of preparation meeting the right moment.

What the masters gave me was not a credential to put on a wall. It gave me a validated framework for the pattern recognition I had already been developing in the field: why some businesses scale and others plateau, why certain markets respond to certain strategies, and how to build infrastructure that survives the founder stepping back. That formal rigour, layered over 8 years of real-world delivery, is the combination you get when you work with me.

"Working with a major international EdTech company as their local representative taught me my north star: I could be the bridge between African talent and international opportunity. That is still exactly what I do."

The Moment It Crystallised

The call I'll never forget.

A funded, press-featured founder on the verge of collapse. Not because the business was failing. Because she was the only person who knew how anything worked. She was drowning in her own success.

That call named what I'd been solving for years. Most African founders stall not from lack of talent, but because they're scaling on hustle instead of structure. 60-hour weeks. Inconsistent revenue. Teams that can't move without you. I fix that.

How I Work

Great results don't come from
one good month.

There is something the consulting industry rarely says out loud. Strategy without consistency is just expensive advice. The clients who get the most transformative results are not the ones with the biggest budgets. They are the ones who show up, do the work repeatedly, and trust the process long enough for it to compound.

🤝

I invest in the relationship, not just the deliverable

I take a small number of engagements deliberately. Not because of capacity, but because the best outcomes come from a consultant who genuinely knows your business, your market, your team dynamics, and your blind spots. That depth takes time and it is worth building.

🔁

Repetition is not redundancy. It is how traction works.

Brand positioning does not land after one campaign. Revenue systems do not convert after one funnel. Authority is not built in a week. The founders who see the biggest results are those who commit to the strategy and run it long enough for the market to respond. I will tell you this upfront so we start aligned.

🎯

I need you to be willing to do the work too

The best engagements are partnerships, not handoffs. I bring the strategy, the systems, and the execution. But I need a client who shows up to calls prepared, makes decisions, and trusts the process. If you want someone to take all responsibility off your plate entirely, I am probably not the right fit. If you want a strategic partner who is all in when you are, I am exactly that.

🏗️

Trust is the infrastructure everything else is built on

I have walked away from clients who paid well but operated in bad faith. I have ended contracts where the working relationship had degraded to the point where good work was no longer possible. This is not a warning. It is a standard. When we work together, you get my full commitment. I ask for honesty and mutual respect in return.

What This Journey Taught Me

The hard-won truths behind
every strategy I build.

Boundaries Are Business Strategy

Negotiation, self-advocacy, and getting things in writing are not soft skills, they are survival skills. Vet people. Set terms. Don't discount your fees. A client who haggles on price will almost always be a client from hell.

Your Business Doesn't Grow Until You Do

Personal development, mindset mastery, and proactively applying what you've learned are the real keys. There are rules in business, and additional rules that uniquely impact Black African women. Mastering both is great leadership.

Entrepreneurship Is Deeply Spiritual

Those who get it will understand what I mean. Align with people who share your mindset. Build communities with them. Lean on your Higher Power. Invest in the practices that make the journey meaningful.
