Scaling the First Afro-Nomadic Community Platform

Building a multi-channel growth engine for Afro Adventurers—achieving in 46 days what takes most community managers 6 months

Timeline: 46 days (Sep 16 - Nov 1, 2025)
Industry: Travel Tech / Community Platform
My Role: Co-Marketing Manager
Value Delivered: $1,500 - $2,695

The Challenge

As a solo founder building the first Afro-nomadic community of its kind, Quiana Berry needed strategic planning and marketing execution to scale membership for Afro Adventurer™. The platform required:

  • • Consistent multi-platform content to drive audience growth
  • • Community engagement systems across WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Instagram, and Facebook
  • • Strategic partnerships to establish credibility in competitive travel tech market
  • • Foundation for monetization through events and membership transition

My Role

Co-Marketing Manager • Brand Strategist • Community Architect • Business Development Lead • Event Coordinator
